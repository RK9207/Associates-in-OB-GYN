## When pain keeps getting in the way

You do not have to wait until pain is unbearable to ask for help. A consultation can be useful when symptoms persist, return, or still do not make sense after previous care.

**Symptom examples**

- Back, neck, or joint pain that limits daily activities
- Pain traveling into an arm or leg
- Numbness, tingling, burning, or weakness
- Headaches or migraines that are not improving
- Pain that continues after therapy, medication, or a prior procedure

**Common concerns we evaluate:** Back and neck pain, sciatica, spinal stenosis, arthritis, neuropathy, headaches, and pain after spine surgery. [These are examples, not a diagnosis or exhaustive condition list.]

## How care is chosen

**Eyebrow:** Treatment options

## A plan based on your pain, not a preset procedure

Dr. Villanueva considers your symptoms, exam, records, imaging, and what you want to be able to do. He explains what may be causing the pain and why a particular next step may help.

**What this gives you:** A clearer picture of possible causes, an explanation of your options, and a plan aimed at better day-to-day function. The goal is meaningful progress, even when completely eliminating pain is not possible.

### Targeted procedures

When a specific pain source is identified, options may include image-guided injections, nerve blocks, radiofrequency ablation, or other interventional treatments. A procedure is recommended only when it fits the findings and your goals.

### Ongoing pain care

Some plans call for diagnostic testing, physical therapy or rehabilitation planning, medication review, lifestyle changes, or coordination with another specialist. Injections are one option, not a requirement.

**Closing line:** If a surgical opinion is worth considering, Dr. Villanueva will discuss that with you.

## Common questions, answered

### Is pain management only for severe pain?

No. If pain persists, limits your activities, or has not improved with standard care, an evaluation may help you understand your options.

### Will I need injections?

Not necessarily. Your plan may involve testing, rehabilitation, medication review, a procedure, or referral. Dr. Villanueva recommends options based on your evaluation.

### What happens at the first visit?

You will discuss your history and symptoms, review available records and imaging, and have an exam. Dr. Villanueva will explain what may be causing your pain and whether more testing is needed.

### Can you help if I still have pain after back surgery?

Pain management may help in many cases. A careful evaluation can look for possible sources of pain and determine which options are appropriate for you.

### What if my MRI does not explain the pain?

Imaging is only one part of the picture. Your symptoms, exam, medical history, and neurological findings can provide other useful clues.

### What insurance or payment options are available?

The clinic lists Medicare, Medi-Medi, and self-pay, and sees personal injury, Workers' Comp, and VA clients. Call **(818) 873-2072** to confirm your specific coverage and next steps.
